# ChickenPick demo note
- **Set up:** plug in a MIDI keyboard and open https://chickenpick.vercel.app in Chrome or Edge. Tap to start audio and pick your keyboard under MIDI in. Use BPM 112, key G major, chords `G C D G`.
- **Play:** press ▶ Play. Hold a G triad (G B D) with your right hand for a bar, then C, D, G. Classic Chicken fires muted ghosts + open twang 16ths on your held tones over the train beat. Ride the **mod wheel** (mute depth) from open down to full mute to bury the ghosts. Sweep **CC74** (cutoff) brighter into the turnaround. Hold **sustain** and switch to Slow Porch for long open twang holds.
- **Suggested patch: "Dusty Tele":**
  - Voice Tele Twang, cutoff ~2.8 kHz, resonance 3.5, attack 4 ms, release 180 ms
  - Recipe Classic Chicken, mute depth 75%, pick length 45%, snap 35%
  - Drums Train beat, scale lock on
  - Switch to Ghost Run for the second 4 bars, then Nashville Hybrid for the tag.
- **Record:** press ● Rec, play 2 passes, press Rec again to keep it, then Export WAV + MIDI and drop both on bar 1.
