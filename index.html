CHICKENPICK

A playable chicken-pickin' Tele for your MIDI keyboard. Hold a chord and tempo-synced muted ghosts, open twang, double-stops and hybrid snaps fire on the grid of your BPM and key, over a country walking bass and train-beat drums.

Tap to start audio.

1 · Your loop

Your BPM, key and chords are saved in this browser and used for playback and export. Changing the key transposes the chords and your take.

2 · Play

bar – · step –

Your MIDI keyboard plays the Tele right away and never starts the transport. When the transport is stopped, you hear the raw Tele voice so you can audition. While playing, the chicken engine picks through held notes (or your take / chord tones). Fallback keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; (Z / X = octave).

3 · Chicken pickin'

Grid codes: M mute ghost · O open twang · D double-stop · S snap accent · H hold · - rest. Click a step to cycle.

4 · Voice + mix

Mix

5 · MIDI + audio

Controls: notes + velocity · CC64 sustain · CC1 mod wheel = chicken mute depth · CC74 = cutoff · pitch bend ±2 · CC123 all notes off. MIDI out mirrors what you play (ch 1), the bass (ch 2) and drums (ch 10). No audio input is used.

6 · Export (drops on bar 1 of your DAW)

48 kHz / 24-bit stereo, exactly your bars at your BPM, downbeat at sample 0, ring-out folded into the start so it loops, peak ≤ −1 dBFS. The WAV plays your take (or your chords held one per bar if you haven't recorded one) through the chicken recipes. MIDI: tempo, 4/4, key, one track per chair (Chicken Tele picks, Country Bass, Train Beat).

Every sound in ChickenPick is synthesized in your browser and named as a synth. No samples, no accounts, no tracking. © 2026 WorkinWithAI.
